NumRush Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 5, 2026

Overview

NumRush is a mental math trainer app. We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about our data practices. The short version: we don't collect any personal data.

Data Collection

NumRush does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. The app operates entirely offline on your device.

Data Storage

All app data — including your scores, settings, and game history — is stored locally on your device using standard device storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Analytics & Tracking

NumRush does not use any analytics services, tracking pixels, advertising SDKs, or third-party services of any kind. There is no telemetry.

Advertising

NumRush does not display advertisements. There are no ad networks or ad-related SDKs in the app.

Third-Party Services

NumRush does not integrate with any third-party services. The app does not make any network requests.

Children's Privacy

NumRush does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us at: unlimitedlearn@proton.me
